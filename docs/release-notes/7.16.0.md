# 7.16.0 (5 November 2026)

One estate with the sister portals, and research in the open (R73, R74). The catalogue now uses the DAM capability playbooks' and the DQ Commons' own keys: #dama=DM-GOV opens a knowledge area on the wheel and #standard=dcat3 a standard's row, in both link forms, and data/xref.json publishes its keys, 1,271 organisations and 54 views in the shape the others use, attested with the page. Related elsewhere now also appears on Data tools and suppliers, and Legacy and technical debt, and a weekly check reads both sister portals' manifests and every page linked to them. New view, Help shape the catalogue: user research in three rounds with tactical and strategic questions, ways to take part, ideas and votes from GitHub, how the backlog is decided by value, reach, strategic fit and feasibility with capacity as a gate, and the backlog itself, checked against the roadmap for modern digital government and the DAM Policy, with the forthcoming Strategic Data Roadmap shown as forthcoming. The research bundle is in docs/research, and build/tools/research_bundle.py makes the version for sending by email. New issue forms: take part in research, suggest an idea, and a survey; only totals are published, and counts under 5 are hidden.
