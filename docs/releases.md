- **7.10.0** (1 Nov 2026): Beyond GitHub: which projects are linked from the UK Government Digital and GDS Slack workspaces and the GDS Google Drive, in which channels, and what about (incidents, releases, security, migrations, questions, dependencies), with the projects live services lean on; each project's profile says where it is talked about.
