# Personas

> **Status:** Proto-personas and assumed journeys, written from the requests that shaped the catalogue. Not yet tested with users: research is the first thing to do next.

## P1: Policy lead

*Policy or strategy colleague in a department*

**Goal:** Find out who in government already works on my policy area, before commissioning anything.

**Needs:** Plain words, no GitHub knowledge; Browse by topic and life event; Who to talk to

**Pain points:** Code projects mean nothing to me; I can't tell what's live from what's abandoned

**Starts at:** Home, then Explore repos or a topic

**Uses:** home, discover, topics, journeys, profile

## P2: Data leader

*Chief Data Officer or data governance lead*

**Goal:** See my organisation's data estate in code: data quality, catalogues, pipelines, legacy and debt.

**Needs:** DAMA view of capabilities; Legacy and debt signals; Compare with peers

**Pain points:** Evidence of data practice is scattered; Legacy risk is invisible until it bites

**Starts at:** Insights and analytics

**Uses:** insights, dama, suppliers, legacy, orgs, family

## P3: Service owner

*Product or service owner building a public service*

**Goal:** Reuse what exists before building: components, APIs, journeys and patterns.

**Needs:** Search everything; APIs and open data; Who maintains it and can I reuse it

**Pain points:** Duplicated builds across departments; Licences and ownership are unclear

**Starts at:** Search everything

**Uses:** search, catalogue, apis, opendata, profile, guidance

## P4: Engineering and security lead

*Head of engineering, architect or security lead*

**Goal:** Check how well our code is looked after and where the risks are.

**Needs:** Good practice across disciplines; Retired technology; GitHub hygiene

**Pain points:** Unowned repositories; Retired libraries still in use

**Starts at:** Good practice

**Uses:** practice, practice2, legacy, gdsway, gdsx

## P5: Repository owner moving from co-cddo

*Owner of a co-cddo repository*

**Goal:** Move my repository to its new home without breaking anything.

**Needs:** What moves where; What breaks when it moves; A decision record

**Pain points:** Hidden dependencies; Links and Pages that silently break

**Starts at:** co-cddo move

**Uses:** start, overview, repos, lineage, safe

## P6: Cross-government architect

*Architect working across GDS and the govukOS apps*

**Goal:** Join this catalogue to GTI, ANA, LIAH and the shared registers on one key.

**Needs:** Organisation crosswalk; Feeds in open formats; Lineage and traceability

**Pain points:** Three organisation id schemes; No register entry

**Starts at:** govukOS connections

**Uses:** govukos, knowledge, family, nations, international
